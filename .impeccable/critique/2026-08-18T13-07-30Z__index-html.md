## Overall Impression

The page opens with confidence and strong editorial control. Its biggest opportunity is to convert breadth into proof: the work section should be the emotional peak but currently presents three unavailable case studies.

## What’s Working

- Oversized serif typography, technical labels, rules, and alternating fields create disciplined pacing.
- Skip navigation, semantic headings, Escape/outside-click dismissal, and reduced-motion intent show interaction care.
- The collaboration path remains lightweight and consistently available.

## Priority Issues

3. **[P1] Mobile navigation removes the information map.** Section links disappear across a very long page. Add a compact accessible section menu.
4. **[P2] The proposition is too broad to be memorable.** Anchor the opening around a distinctive intersection such as software meeting physical systems, then make breadth secondary.
5. **[P2] Motion is plentiful but uniform.** Preserve one authored hero entrance and shorten repetitive reveal choreography.
